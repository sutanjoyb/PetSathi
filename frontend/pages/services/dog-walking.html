THE BENEFITS

Why Dog Walking Matters

Walking is more than exercise. It supports your dog's physical health, mental wellbeing, and everyday happiness.

Physical Fitness

Regular movement supports healthy muscles, joints, stamina, and cardiovascular fitness.

Mental Wellness

New sights, sounds, and scents provide stimulation and help prevent boredom.

Weight Management

Consistent activity helps dogs maintain a healthy weight alongside balanced nutrition.

Socialisation

Positive outdoor experiences help dogs become more comfortable around people and other dogs.

Better Sleep

Appropriate daytime activity can help dogs settle down and maintain a healthy routine.

Reduced Boredom

Exploration and activity provide a positive outlet for energy and curiosity.

EXPLORE THE OPTIONS

Find the Right Kind of Walk

Different dogs have different routines. Choose a walking style that suits your companion.

Daily Walks

Build a consistent routine with regular outdoor activity and exploration.

Morning Walks

Start the day with fresh air and an energising outdoor experience.

Evening Walks

Enjoy a calmer outing when temperatures are more comfortable.

Puppy Walks

Introduce short, gentle outings that suit a puppy's age and development.

Senior Dog Walks

Keep outings comfortable and gentle according to mobility and health.

Individual Walks

Give your dog focused attention with a walk tailored to its needs.

A ROUTINE THAT FITS

How Long Should Your Dog Walk?

There is no single duration that works for every dog. Consider age, breed, health, fitness, and energy levels.

Puppies

Short, gentle outings with plenty of rest and time to explore.

Adult Dogs

Regular walks adjusted to activity needs, fitness, and breed.

Senior Dogs

Comfortable, low-impact walks with breaks whenever needed.

Small Breeds

Manageable walks based on stamina, size, and individual needs.

Medium Breeds

Balanced activity suited to energy levels and daily routine.

Large Breeds

Appropriate exercise while considering joint health and endurance.

Consult your veterinarian for an exercise routine suited to your dog's age, health, breed, and individual requirements. Adjust walks according to your dog's comfort and energy.
BE WALK-READY

Essential Walking Accessories

A few simple essentials can make outdoor adventures safer and more comfortable.

Leash

Maintain control and guide your dog safely during walks.

Harness

Choose a well-fitted harness that supports comfortable movement.

Collar & ID Tag

Keep identification details accessible in case your dog gets lost.

Water Bottle

Carry fresh water to keep your companion hydrated.

Poop Bags

Clean up after your dog and keep shared spaces tidy.

Reflective Gear

Improve visibility during early morning and evening walks.

SAFETY FIRST

Make Every Walk a Safe Walk

A little preparation helps protect your dog and everyone sharing the walking route.

01

Secure the Leash and Harness

Check that all equipment fits properly and is securely fastened.

02

Avoid Extreme Heat

Choose cooler hours and shorten or skip walks during excessive heat.

03

Carry Fresh Water

Offer water regularly, especially on longer or warmer outings.

04

Check Pavement Temperature

Test the walking surface before heading out to protect sensitive paws.

05

Choose Safe Routes

Avoid heavy traffic, unsafe areas, and places where dogs are not permitted.

06

Watch for Exhaustion

Stop and rest if your dog shows excessive panting, weakness, or discomfort.

07

Clean Up Responsibly

Always dispose of waste properly and respect shared public spaces.

WALK THROUGH EVERY SEASON

Weather-Wise Walking Tips

Adjust your routine to the weather and your dog's comfort.

Summer

Walk during cooler hours, avoid hot surfaces, and carry enough water.

Monsoon

Prefer safer paths, avoid flooded areas, and dry your dog after walks.

Winter

Consider your dog's cold tolerance and use suitable protection when needed.
